Our Story

From the Himalayas to practical AI education.

Harish Singh built HUMAIXO to remove the usual barriers around distance, cost, and confusion, and to give learners a clean path into AI, startups, and digital careers.

This is more than a course site. It is a learning ecosystem where learners from Uttarakhand and beyond can study with purpose, keep notes organized, and turn skills into real output.

The platform is designed to grow with the learner: start with one course, build one habit, and expand into a stronger career or business path over time.
